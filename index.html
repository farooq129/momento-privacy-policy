Privacy Policy

Memento Note for Android · Effective 6 November 2026

The short version

  • Memento Note does not collect, store or share any personal data on any server.
  • Your notes, journal entries, ideas, goals and images are saved only on your device.
  • There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Memento Note mobile app (package com.momentonote.rfapp.app), published on Google Play by its independent developer ("we", "us"). It explains how the app handles your information.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app has no sign-up, no user accounts and no servers that receive your content.

3. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Memento Note is saved in a local database and folder on your phone, including:

  • notebooks, notes, checklists, journal entries, ideas and tags
  • goals and their steps
  • images you choose to add to your notes
  • app settings, such as your theme (Light, Dark or Study)

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself. We cannot see, access or recover it.

4. Backups

The Backup feature, including optional automatic backups, saves a copy of your data in the app's storage on your device. Backups are not uploaded to us. If your device has Android's own system backup turned on, Android may include app data in your Google account backup. That process is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by Memento Note.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Photos / storageOnly used when you choose to add an image to a note. The image you pick is copied into the app's private storage on your device.
Camera, microphoneListed by system components the app is built with. Memento Note does not record audio and does not take photos in the background. Android will ask for your permission before either could ever be used.

6. Sharing

When you tap Share on a note, the note's text is passed to the app you select, for example email or messaging. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy. Memento Note shares nothing automatically.

7. Third parties, ads and analytics

Memento Note contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

8. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

9. Your control and deleting your data

You can edit or delete any note, notebook, goal or backup inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Memento Note, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes all of its data from your device.

10. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. To keep it safe, use a screen lock on your device and make regular backups.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example if a new feature such as cloud sync is added, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/farooq129/momento-privacy-policy/issues.
